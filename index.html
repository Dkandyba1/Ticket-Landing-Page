FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our custom ticketing systems

How long does implementation take? +
Most projects are completed within 4-6 weeks from initial consultation to launch. The timeline depends on your specific requirements, integrations, and customization needs. We provide a detailed project plan during discovery with clear milestones and deliverables.
What does a custom system cost? +
Investment varies based on features, complexity, and integrations. Standard implementations start at £5,500, while enterprise solutions with advanced automation and custom integrations range from £12,000-£25,000. We offer flexible payment plans and provide detailed quotes after understanding your specific needs.
Do you provide hosting and ongoing support? +
Yes. We offer fully managed hosting with 99.9% uptime SLA, automatic backups, security updates, and 24/7 monitoring. Support packages start at £250/month and include priority assistance, bug fixes, performance optimization, and feature enhancements.
Can you integrate with our existing tools? +
Absolutely. We integrate with popular platforms including Slack, Microsoft Teams, QuickBooks, Salesforce, Stripe, and most CRM/ERP systems. We also provide REST API access for custom integrations with any platform or internal system you use.
What if we need changes after launch? +
We build systems designed to evolve with your business. Minor updates and bug fixes are included in your support plan. For new features or major enhancements, we offer hourly rates (£95/hour) or project-based pricing. Your system grows as your needs change.
Do we own the code and data? +
Yes, complete ownership. Once the project is complete and paid in full, you receive full rights to the source code, database, and all intellectual property. You can self-host or transfer to any provider. There's no vendor lock-in, and you maintain complete control over your data.
